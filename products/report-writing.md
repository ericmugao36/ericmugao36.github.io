## Turn your information into a clear, professional report

A good report does more than collect information. It organizes facts, explains important findings and presents information in a way that helps readers understand the subject.

I provide report writing and editing support for individuals, businesses and organizations that need clear, structured and professional documents.

Whether you have rough notes, existing material or a report that needs improvement, the work begins by understanding its purpose and intended audience.

## Types of reports I can help with

- **Business reports:** Present business activities, findings and recommendations.
- **Project reports:** Document project progress, activities, outcomes and completion.
- **Activity reports:** Summarize meetings, events, programmes and organizational activities.
- **Performance reports:** Present agreed performance information and results.
- **Education-related reports:** Organize school, training and education project information.
- **Research summaries:** Present supplied research information and findings clearly.
- **Meeting and event reports:** Record important discussions, decisions and activities.

## Writing and editing services

Depending on your needs, support can include:

- Structuring and organizing report sections.
- Drafting reports from supplied information.
- Improving clarity, grammar and readability.
- Editing and proofreading existing documents.
- Formatting headings, tables and sections.
- Organizing findings and recommendations.
- Preparing documents in an agreed format.

## What you receive

The final deliverable may include:

- A properly structured report.
- Clear and consistent language.
- Organized findings and supporting information.
- Tables or other presentation elements where appropriate.
- An edited or proofread document.
- A final file in the agreed format.

The deliverables are agreed before work begins.

## Our working approach

1. **Discuss the purpose:** Explain why the report is needed and who will read it.
2. **Share the material:** Provide your notes, records, findings or existing draft.
3. **Agree on the scope:** Confirm the length, format, deadline and price.
4. **Prepare the report:** The document is drafted, organized or edited.
5. **Review and deliver:** Agreed revisions are addressed before final handover.
