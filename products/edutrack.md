## Help parents understand their children's progress

EduTrack is a school assessment dashboard designed to help schools organize learner assessment results and make academic progress easier for parents to understand.

### What it offers

- Student assessment records and reports.
- Teacher comments on learners' academic progress.
- Parent access to their children's reports.
- School and teacher management tools.
- A more organized way to communicate academic progress.
