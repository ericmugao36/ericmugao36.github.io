## Turn raw data into useful information

Spreadsheets and business records often contain information that is difficult to interpret in its original form.

Data analysis helps organize that information, identify useful patterns and present findings in a format that supports better understanding and decision-making.

I help individuals, small businesses and organizations turn raw or disorganized data into clear reports and practical summaries.

## What I can help with

### Data cleaning and preparation

- Organizing Excel spreadsheets.
- Identifying missing or inconsistent entries.
- Checking and handling duplicate records.
- Standardizing dates, categories and other fields.
- Preparing data for analysis.

### Business data analysis

- Sales and revenue analysis.
- Expense summaries.
- Profit and performance analysis where cost data is available.
- Product and service comparisons.
- Branch or department performance comparisons.
- Identification of patterns and trends.

### Reports and visualizations

- Summary tables and charts.
- Monthly and periodic performance reports.
- Business performance dashboards.
- Comparisons across products, categories or locations.
- Visual summaries that make findings easier to understand.

### Automated reporting

Where suitable, Python can be used to process data and prepare repeatable reports.

## Tools and formats

Depending on the project, I may use:

- Microsoft Excel.
- Python and Pandas.
- Power BI.
- Matplotlib.
- Structured CSV and spreadsheet files.

The tools selected depend on the dataset, reporting objectives and expected deliverables.

A project may include:
