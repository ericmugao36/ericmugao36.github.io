## Give your business a professional online presence

A good website helps potential customers understand what you offer, explore your services and find a reliable way to contact you.

I create practical websites for small businesses, freelancers and professionals who want to establish or improve their presence online.

Whether you need a business website, personal portfolio, CV website or blog, the project is planned around your content, audience and objectives.

## What your website can include

Depending on the agreed project scope:

- **Home page:** Introduce your business, work or professional profile.
- **About page:** Explain who you are and what you do.
- **Services or products:** Present your offerings clearly.
- **Portfolio or projects:** Showcase previous work and examples.
- **Contact information:** Give visitors a straightforward way to reach you.
- **Mobile-friendly layout:** Make the website usable on phones, tablets and computers.
- **Search-friendly structure:** Use meaningful page titles, descriptions and organized content.
- **Publishing assistance:** Help make the completed website accessible online.

Additional features can be discussed and quoted separately.
